# Vendor Risk Assessment Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA) conducted as part of a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) cybersecurity exercise.

The objective was to evaluate a third-party cloud storage vendor before onboarding them to determine whether they pose any security, compliance, operational, or data privacy risks to the organization.

## Scope

The assessment covered:

- Vendor security controls
- Data protection practices
- Incident response capabilities
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Employee security awareness training
- Access management controls
- Compliance considerations

## Assessment Methodology

The assessment followed a standard Vendor Risk Assessment process:

1. Vendor identification
2. Security questionnaire review
3. Risk identification
4. Risk scoring and classification
5. Recommendation development
6. Final approval decision

## Risk Categories Assessed

- Information Security Risk
- Data Privacy Risk
- Operational Risk
- Compliance Risk
- Third-Party Risk

## Deliverables

The project includes:

- Vendor Information Sheet
- Security Questionnaire
- Risk Register
- Risk Scoring Matrix
- Recommendations and Mitigation Plan
- Final Assessment Summary

## Key Findings

Several areas of potential concern were identified, including gaps in employee security awareness training and weaknesses in password management practices.

Appropriate remediation recommendations were provided to reduce overall vendor risk exposure.

## Outcome

Based on the assessment findings, the vendor was classified according to its overall risk profile and provided with recommendations for risk mitigation before full approval.
